Read this first

TikTok has no attribution anywhere in our stack. We checked every custom attribute name and value in Braze across 189 recent profiles, every campaign, canvas and segment name, all Braze tags, all 43 Everflow affiliates, and every Everflow sub-id. There are zero references to TikTok. Traffic source is stored only as a numeric Everflow affiliate ID, so a TikTok visitor is currently indistinguishable from an untracked direct visitor. Nothing in this report can confirm or rule out TikTok's contribution. It can only show what moved, and what tracked sources do and do not explain.

The claim, and what the numbers say

The agency's position is that their traffic raises quiz intake volume, that a share of that audience abandons before checkout, and that those abandoners are recoverable through the SMS and outbound recovery programme.

The first two links in that chain are consistent with the data. Abandon cart events grew +46% between the two 30-day halves of this window, from 231/day to 337/day. Purchases grew +115% over the same period. Something is genuinely driving more intake.

The attribution link cannot be tested. Abandon volume grew roughly twice as fast as tracked clicks, +46% against +23%. Holding the prior period's abandon-per-click rate constant, tracked traffic predicts about 8,543 abandons in the last 30 days. We recorded 10,106. That leaves roughly 1,563 abandons, about 52 a day, that tracked sources do not account for.

That gap is consistent with untracked traffic arriving on site, which is what TikTok would look like today given no tracking links exist for it. It is equally consistent with the on-site funnel simply converting visitors to quiz starts at a higher rate than before. Both explanations fit, and the current setup cannot separate them.

What would settle the question

One change makes the agency's claim measurable rather than arguable:

Create a TikTok affiliate in Everflow and route all TikTok ad traffic through its tracking link. Every visitor then carries an affiliate ID that lands on the Braze profile as affid, exactly as Meta, Google and the others already do. From that point on, TikTok-sourced quiz intakes, abandons, recoveries and revenue are all directly countable, with no inference required.

Until that exists, the 1,563-abandon gap can be argued either way and neither side can prove it.
