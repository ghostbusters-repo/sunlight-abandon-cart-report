What this can and cannot answer

TikTok has no tracking anywhere in our stack. We checked every Braze custom attribute name and value across 189 recent profiles, every campaign, canvas and segment name, all Braze tags, all 43 Everflow affiliates, and every Everflow sub-id. Zero references. Traffic source is stored only as a numeric Everflow affiliate ID, so a TikTok visitor is currently indistinguishable from an untracked direct visitor. This report cannot confirm or rule out TikTok's contribution. It shows what moved, and what tracked sources do and do not explain.

The claim, and what traffic data says

The agency's position is that their traffic raises quiz intake volume, and that the share of that audience which bails before checkout is recoverable downstream.

Intake volume is genuinely up, and by more than traffic alone accounts for. Tracked clicks rose +21% between the first and last three complete weeks. Quiz abandons rose +53% over the same period, roughly two and a half times faster.

Put another way, the site is producing 77.1 abandons per 1,000 tracked clicks, against 60.9 seven weeks ago, a +27% improvement. Holding the old rate constant, tracked traffic predicts about 5,628 abandons in the last three weeks. We recorded 7,126, leaving roughly 1,498, about 71 a day, that tracked sources do not account for.

Two explanations fit equally well. Untracked traffic could be arriving on site and producing quiz intakes without producing tracked clicks, which is exactly what TikTok would look like today. Or the on-site funnel could simply be converting visitors to quiz starts at a better rate than before. The current setup cannot separate them.

One tracked source is worth noting before attributing the gap to anything untracked: CompareTreatments grew from 937 clicks a week to 5,579, up 495%, and now supplies about a fifth of tracked traffic. A material share of the intake growth sits there and is fully accounted for.

What would settle it

One change makes the claim measurable rather than arguable:

Create a TikTok affiliate in Everflow and route all TikTok ad traffic through its tracking link. Every visitor then carries an affiliate ID that lands on the Braze profile as affid, exactly as Meta, Google, Microsoft and the rest already do. From that point TikTok-sourced clicks, quiz intakes and abandons are directly countable, with no inference required.

Until that exists, the 1,498-abandon gap can be argued either way and neither side can prove it.

Sources: Braze REST API /events/data_series for the Abandoned Cart event, and Everflow Network API /v1/networks/reporting/entity for clicks by affiliate. Weeks run Wednesday to Tuesday.
